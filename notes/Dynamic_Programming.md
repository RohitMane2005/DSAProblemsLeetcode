### 1D & 2D DP: Memoization, Tabulation & Space Optimization
- Identified subproblem recurrence: dp[i] = max(dp[i-1], dp[i-2] + val[i]).
- Optimized space from O(N) to O(1) keeping running state variables.
- Time: O(N), Space: O(1).
